# Research Reports and Academic Publications (`reports/`)

This directory contains the formal epidemiological research protocol, executive briefing, data provenance records, schema drift audit logs, academic poster assets, and graduate fellowship statement.

## Directory Overview

* **Open Science Standards:** Adheres to preregistered observational study guidelines, documenting hypotheses and analytical plans before model evaluation.
* **Complete Data Provenance:** Cryptographically documents CDC NHANES data sources with direct URLs and SHA-256 hashes.
* **Publication Deliverables:** Includes conference-ready academic poster files (HTML, vector PDF, and PNG) and automated schema drift audit logs.

## File Summary

| File | Document Type | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| `fellowship_statement_draft.md` | Fellowship Statement | Technical research defense and application statement for graduate fellowships and research appointments |

## Detailed File Guide

### `protocol.md` (Epidemiological Research Protocol)
* **What it covers:** Outlines the study framework: research objectives, target population, exclusion criteria, feature harmonization, model configurations, and pre-specified primary endpoints (sensitivity at 20% budget, 61-patient margin swap, and Decision Curve Net Monetary Benefit).
* **Role:** Establishes methodological transparency by documenting the study plan prior to model execution.

### `brief.md` (Executive Research Briefing)
* **What it covers:** Provides a concise executive summary written for health economists, clinical leadership, and technical recruiters.
* **Key insights:** Explains how administrative proxy labels misclassify undiagnosed patients who rarely visit clinics, and highlights the $25,500 net economic dividend per 10,000 screened individuals yielded by biomarker retraining.

### `sources.md` (CDC NHANES Data Provenance)
* **What it covers:** Full provenance catalog for all 10 SAS transport (.XPT) files ingested from the CDC NHANES portal:
  * Pre-pandemic 2017 to March 2020: `P_DEMO`, `P_DIQ`, `P_GHB`, `P_BMX`, `P_HIQ`.
  * Post-pandemic 2021 to 2023: `DEMO_L`, `DIQ_L`, `GHB_L`, `BMX_L`, `HIQ_L`.
* **Role:** Ensures end-to-end reproducibility and enables automated integrity verification via SHA-256 checksums.

### `data_dictionary.csv` (Variable Crosswalk)
* **What it covers:** Maps raw CDC survey codes (such as `DIQ010`, `LBXGH`, `BMXBMI`, `INDFMPIR`) to clean analytical feature names, descriptions, and measurement units.

### `schema_drift_audit.json` and `schema_drift_audit.md` (Data Quality Audit Logs)
* **What it covers:** Machine-readable and markdown logs generated by the Pandera schema validation gate:
  * Missingness change across BMI, Waist Circumference, and PIR remained under 1.0%.
  * Physical covariates showed high stability: Age (PSI = 0.0309), BMI (PSI = 0.0195), Waist (PSI = 0.0175).
  * Documented the expected clinical downward shift in HbA1c (PSI = 0.275) when restricting the evaluation cohort to undiagnosed adults.

### `poster.html`, `poster.pdf`, and `poster.png` (Academic Research Poster)
* **What it covers:** 36x24 inch academic poster formatted on a 4-column balanced grid with Newsreader serif headings and clinical sapphire figures.
* **Role:** Ready for presentation at medical informatics conferences and printable in vector PDF format.

### `fellowship_statement_draft.md` (Graduate Fellowship & Research Statement)
* **What it covers:** Technical research statement draft for graduate fellowships, academic research labs, and biostatistics appointments, synthesizing econometric access theory with empirical biostatistics.
